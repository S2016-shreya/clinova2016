## Development Roadmap — Homingo Hackathon 2026

**Project:** Clinova AI  
**Team:** 2016S  
**Track:** Automation (Proposed)

### Current Status — Working Prototype

Clinova AI has an operational prototype featuring participant registration, duplicate-entry prevention, multilingual research questionnaires, data-quality monitoring, researcher dashboards, and downloadable reports.

Our development strategy is to build upon this existing foundation rather than develop a new application from scratch.

### Phase 2 — Advanced Research Integrity Automation

**Planned development: 28 November – 3 December 2026**

We aim to implement three major enhancements:

**1. Automated Fraud-Risk Detection**

Introduce rule-based validation to identify suspicious submissions, inconsistent responses, and potential duplicate records before they affect downstream research analysis.

**2. Real-Time Researcher Alerts**

Develop a notification system that alerts authorized researchers when a submission requires verification or manual review.

**3. Transparent Automation Activity Logs**

Create an auditable history of data-quality checks, flagged submissions, researcher decisions, and system actions.

Together, these enhancements aim to make research data verification more proactive, measurable, and transparent.

### Phase 3 — Demonstration and Submission

**Target: 4 December 2026**

### Our Commitment

We are not proposing an idea without a technical foundation. We have already developed a functional research management prototype and established a focused development plan for expanding its automation capabilities.

Our goal is to demonstrate how accessible, human-supervised automation can improve the reliability and efficiency of clinical research data workflows.
